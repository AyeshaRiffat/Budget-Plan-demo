1. Getting started (first 5 minutes)

Budget & Plan tracks money in, money out, bills, goals and debts. Everything is stored on this device only. Do these steps once and the whole app comes alive:

  1. Open Settings (⚙ in the sidebar footer link “Backup / Restore data”, or Quick actions → Settings) and choose your currency, number style and a colour theme.
  2. Bank Accounts → Add account: create each place you keep money (e.g. Main Bank, Cash, Savings) with its current real balance.
  3. Recurring & Payments → Add: enter rent, electricity, internet, subscriptions with their due day. This is what makes “Safe to spend today” accurate.
  4. Log your first spend from the sidebar Quick log. You just earned your first badge 🌱.
  5. Optionally set a Monthly Budget per category and create a Sinking Fund for something you’re saving toward.
Come back daily for 30 seconds: log what you spent. Streaks, badges and an accurate daily limit all come from that one habit.
2. Layout & navigation

Left sidebar (always visible)

Logo, your 🔥 logging streak, theme colour dots, every page, the Quick log panel and the backup link. It stays fixed while the page scrolls. On phones and tablets it becomes a drawer: tap ☰ at the top to open it, tap outside to close.

Month tabs (right edge)

JAN–DEC coloured tabs fixed to the right edge. The current period is the purple tab that slides out. Click any month to jump to the Monthly Budget for that month; the 50/30/20 page follows the same month. On phones the tabs sit in a strip along the bottom.

Quick actions (Ctrl + K)

Press Ctrl K (or ⌘ K on Mac), or click “Quick actions”, to open a menu that adds a transaction or jumps to any page without hunting through the sidebar.

Focus mode

The toggle at the top of Today hides everything except the greeting, the “one thing” banner, your safe-to-spend card and the three small things. Use it when you only want to know “what do I do right now?”.

Status bar

The thin bar at the bottom of the screen (desktop) shows the month, days left and how much is still yours after upcoming bills.

Phone shortcut

On small screens a round ＋ button floats above the month strip; tap it to add a full transaction quickly.

3. Today dashboard

Safe to spend today

The big number is how much you can spend today without hurting the rest of the month. Formula: (all account balances − bills not yet paid this month + what you already spent today) ÷ days left including today, minus what you’ve spent today. If it shows 0 your balances don’t cover your unpaid bills — check accounts and bills. The bar fills as you spend and turns red if you pass today’s starting amount. The three chips (Coffee, Lunch, Bus) log a preset spend in one tap.

Right now · one thing only

The dark banner shows the first unfinished task. Press Done — next to mark it finished and see the next one. Finish all three for a confetti celebration 🎉.

Three small things

A daily checklist: (1) log anything you spent — ticks itself when you’ve logged today, (2) check your balance matches your bank, (3) keep today under your limit. Click any row to tick or untick it. Tap ＋ Add your own to add personal daily tasks (e.g. “Check gold rate”); they repeat every day and ✕ removes them. Everything resets every day.

What you logged

Today’s transactions, newest first. Click a row to open it, edit it or delete it.

Brain dump

A scratch space for non-money thoughts (“call the landlord”, “renew passport”). Type and press Enter; ✕ removes a note. Notes are saved on the device.

Coming up

Bills you haven’t marked paid this month, ordered by due day.

Every day this month

One bar per day showing how much you spent. Taller = more. Today is outlined, future days are faded. Hover or tap a bar for the exact amount.

Summary cards & charts

  • Total balance – all accounts together. Income / Expenses – this month, with a 6-month sparkline. Savings rate – (income − expenses) ÷ income.
  • Income vs Expenses – last 6 months side by side. Spending by Category – donut chart of this month. Account Balances and Recent Transactions.

Milestones

Your badges — see section 14.

4. Quick log (sidebar)

The fastest way to record an expense. Type the exact amount (or tap the quick keys; ⟲ clears it), pick a category button — More categories shows all of them, Edit these buttons opens Settings — then optionally change Which day (to log something you forgot) and add a Note. Press Add to today (or Enter). The button stays faded until you type an amount.

On desktop this card lives in the sidebar. On phones and tablets, tap the round ＋ button or any “Log a spend” button and the same card opens as a popup with a Close button. The expense is saved to your first account and its balance drops immediately.

Need a different account or want to record income? Use the full + Add Transaction form (Quick actions → Add a full transaction).
5. Transactions

The complete history. + Add Transaction asks for: type (income or expense), date, amount, account, category (or “+ Add new category…”) and an optional note.

  • Use the search box and the account, category and type filters to find anything.
  • Edit / delete with the row buttons. Account balances are corrected automatically — editing an amount reverses the old one and applies the new one.
  • Income increases an account, expenses decrease it.
6. Bank accounts

Create an account for every place money lives. Types: Checking, Savings, Cash, Credit Card. Each has a name and a balance. The page shows totals and a card per account with edit/delete.

Balances move automatically when you add, edit or delete transactions. If your real bank balance differs, edit the account and enter the true figure (that’s task 2 in “Three small things”).
7. Recurring & payments

List every repeating payment: name, amount, due day, frequency (Monthly, Weekly, Yearly, or ＋ Custom repeat… such as “2 weeks” or “3 months”), category and the account it’s paid from.

  • Mark paid – ticks the bill for this month and automatically creates an expense transaction and reduces that account. Untick if you marked it by mistake — the expense it created is removed and the balance is restored.
  • This month’s list, a due-date calendar, a chart, and the full list of all bills are on this page.
  • Unpaid bills are subtracted when calculating Safe to spend.
8. Monthly budget

Compare what you planned with what you really spent, for any month. Use the ‹ › arrows or the right-edge month tabs to change month.

  1. Press Edit budgets and enter a limit for each category.
  2. The chart and table show budget vs actual, the difference, and a progress bar per category. Bars turn red when you overspend.
9. 50/30/20 dashboard

A classic rule: 50% of income on needs, 30% on wants, 20% to savings. Each category is tagged Need / Want / Savings in Settings. The page totals your month’s expenses by tag, compares them with the 50/30/20 targets of your income and shows an expense-distribution chart.

If a category lands in the wrong block, change its type in Settings → Categories.
10. Sinking funds

Save small amounts on purpose for things you know are coming (Eid shopping, laptop, annual insurance). Create a fund with a name, target amount, amount already saved and an optional target date.

  • Use the quick +1,000 and +5,000 buttons on a fund to add saved money (use Edit for any other amount or to correct it); the progress bar animates.
  • When saved reaches the target you get confetti and the Goal Getter badge 🎯.
11. Debt calculator

Add each debt with its balance, yearly interest rate and minimum payment. Then choose a strategy and an extra monthly payment.

  • Snowball – pay the smallest balance first (fast wins, motivating).
  • Avalanche – pay the highest interest first (cheapest overall).

The page simulates month by month and shows the payoff order, the debt-free date, total interest and a balance-over-time chart.

12. Net worth

Net worth = assets − liabilities. Add a snapshot (date, total assets, total liabilities) whenever you like — monthly works well. You get the current figure, the change since the previous snapshot, a line chart and a bar chart over time, and a history list you can delete from.

13. No-spend challenge

Choose a goal of days this month with no spending (default 20). Every day with no expense logged counts automatically. You can override a day by clicking it: first click confirms it as no-spend, second marks it as “spent”, third returns it to automatic.

The page shows days achieved against your goal, a calendar and a trend chart. Five no-spend days in a month earns No-Spend Ninja 🧘, and confirming a day triggers a small celebration.

15. Settings & themes
  • Currency – pick from the list (PKR, USD, EUR, GBP, INR, AED, SAR and more); the symbol is filled in for you and you can edit it freely for a custom currency.
  • Number style – South Asian grouping (12,50,000), international (1,250,000) or European (1.250.000). Decimals – show whole numbers or 2 decimal places. A live preview shows the result.
  • Colour theme – Lavender, Maroon, Dark green, Midnight (dark) and Sunset. Tap the dots in the sidebar or the swatches in Settings; the choice is remembered.
  • Categories – rename, retype (Need/Want/Savings) or delete; new ones appear everywhere instantly. Deleting a category does not delete its old transactions.
  • Accounts – quick overview and a shortcut to add one.

Press Save settings to apply currency and category changes (themes apply instantly).

16. Backup, restore & privacy

Nothing is uploaded: data lives in your browser’s local storage on this device. That also means a different phone, a different browser, or clearing browser data starts empty.

  • Download backup saves a .json file with everything. Do this regularly (e.g. monthly).
  • Restore from backup loads that file on any device.
  • Reset all data erases everything — download a backup first.
17. Troubleshooting & tips
  • Safe to spend shows 0 – balances are lower than unpaid bills, or accounts have no balance yet. Update balances or mark paid bills as paid.
  • Numbers look wrong – edit the account to the true balance; check there are no duplicate transactions.
  • Spend landed in the wrong account – Quick log always uses your first account; use the full form for others.
  • Streak reset – a full day with no transactions breaks it; log even a tiny spend or income.
  • Lost data – restore your latest backup file.
  • Keyboard – Ctrl/⌘ K quick actions, Enter adds in Quick log and Brain dump, Esc/click outside closes windows.
